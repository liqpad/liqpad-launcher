# Liqpad Mission

Liqpad turns swaps into creator revenue and protocol-owned Venice capacity while B20 fees reduce supply.

| Swap value | Destination | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B20 fee | Burned immediately by the hook | Deflationary public good; never private inventory |
| 70% of collected VVV | Recorded launch creator | Creator revenue, claimable only by that creator |
| 30% of collected VVV | DiemEngine | Protocol-owned Venice capital |

DiemEngine stakes protocol VVV into sVVV, locks eligible sVVV to mint DIEM at Venice's live rate, and may stake DIEM. Staked DIEM provides protocol-owned Venice inference capacity for Liqpad's agent, launch tooling, support, and optional creator compute boosts.

The engine is owner-controlled, not trustless. Harvesting is permissionless, but a multisig owner controls bounded harvest policy, pause, and exceptional timelocked unwind. That control never extends to creator claims, the fee constants, B20 burning, the 70/30 split, VVV-only pools, or permanently locked LP positions.
